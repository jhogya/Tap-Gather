Our Story

Marketing is a fast-moving industry, and we've spent our careers learning how to stay ahead of it — building the brand stories and marketing engines behind companies like ThredUp, The RealReal, Athleta, Therabody, and tvScientific. Along the way, we kept seeing the same gap: businesses with a real story to tell, and no one connecting it to actual growth. That's why we started Tap & Gather — to bring that same caliber of thinking to the businesses right here in Orange County that deserve it just as much.
